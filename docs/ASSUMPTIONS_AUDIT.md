# Arbitrary finite size and residual assumptions

Audited baseline `58f2037d97e476ccd3cd2e189b5efd557b0d1290` on `main`.
Paper scope follows [arXiv v4](https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.10035v4) and the
[repository crosswalk](PAPER_CONFORMANCE.md). Here, completeness means
coverage of the stated result or procedure on its specified domain.
A checker may be sound whenever it returns evidence and still be
inconclusive on some inputs.

## Termination, depth and verdicts

| Assumption and location | Classification and justification | Effect and disposition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `starFuel`, `saturateStar` and `boundedStar` in `NetKAT/Evaluator`; dup-free history execution in `HistoryEvaluator` | **Algorithmic termination precondition**. The product of field sizes equals the cardinality of the packet type by `starFuel_eq_card`. Starting from a singleton, strict monotone growth cannot continue for more rounds than this cardinal. `reachableWithin_iff_at_card` connects the cardinality stage to arbitrary finite iteration. The history evaluator reuses packet execution and preserves the unchanged history tail. | `execute_correct` proves exhaustive output membership for each supplied input. A standalone smaller `boundedStar` computes that smaller relation only. The generic evaluator derives the bound and stops early at equality. No arbitrary exhaustion verdict. |
| Symbolic star in `KATLean/NetKAT/Closure` and its algebraic consumer in `NetKAT/Normalization` | **Algorithmic termination precondition**. Squaring adds accepted valuations. `rank_square_lt` proves strict decrease of the proof-only count of rejected valuations when the canonical diagram changes. `close_spec` establishes minimal closure and `star_correct` gives exact star. | No runtime valuation count or user budget. Finite diagram width justifies the well-founded measure. The separate `boundedPolicy` helper denotes an explicit finite unrolling. Algebraic normalization proves equality at the symbolic fixed point using `derives_star_of_fixed`. Keep. |
| `pairBound` in `KATLean/Native/Decision` and `Explicit` | **Algorithmic termination precondition**. Pair states are subsets of two finite derivative supports. ## Paper comparison and follow-up

- **Already justified.** Finite packet domains, typed guarded source programs, ground scope for v4 Theorem 1, local image finiteness in AIP, and finite specifications with alphabet and visibility premises. The paper's finite packet-domain assumption supplies no universal size bound. The 931- and 1,344-switch figures describe benchmark instances.
- **Temporary implementation restriction.** Finite name support and correct executable restriction classifiers gate graph compilation and exact finite projection. Simpler head/tail and restriction-elimination APIs have their own syntactic fragment premises. The arbitrary source SOS covers those constructs independently. Finite derivative closure or a finite certificate is justified for the corresponding all-depth algorithms only. There is no project-wide finite-state premise.
- **Future generalization.** Thread the schema through the concrete graph and equational layers above, preserving policy-label identity, restriction membership, exact move and projection proofs. Lift Eq. (27)/(28) checkers and certificate consumers once those dependencies are generic. FatTree encoding and the paper's endpoint relation inclusion claim remain separate from this audit.
- **Correctness blocker.** No arbitrary numeric ceiling or budget-derived false semantic verdict was found in the generic paths audited. The concrete API types are a blocker for running the remaining certified analyses on FatTree. They do not justify asserting arbitrary-schema coverage for those analyses today.

`test_packet_schemas.lean` supplies `arbitrary_switch_identifiers` and
`arbitrary_switch_equivalence` and `arbitrary_switch_projection` for any `N : Nat`, including zero. Together
with `compileSymbolic_correct`, `netkatNativeEquivFinite_correct`,
`normalize_derives`, `execute_correct`, `Step.preserves_paperAdmissible`,
`Program.trace_iff_reaches_residual` and the arbitrary-schema source safety
and reachability interfaces, this is theorem-level evidence, independent
of large numerical test runs.

The existing `test_packet_dependencies.lean` guards the transitive
dependencies as well. It rejects the concrete packet cardinality lemma in
the audited core and complete-pair expansion or execution in the symbolic
decision. It also checks that projection derivation avoids closure discovery
and that the word checker uses exact supplied-packet execution.
